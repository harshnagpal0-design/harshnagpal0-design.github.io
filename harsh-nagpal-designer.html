How I work

From brief to launch

Misfits was four founders and an idea. I went to the meetups first, then designed the app around them.

Make it work, then make it feel right, down to the states nobody plans for.

I organised Misfits around clubs, meetups and members, so people find what they came for.

Misfits has a member app and a leader app. I designed every screen of both.

There was no design system, so I built one: colour, type and components, in light and dark.

I start before the brief and stay after handoff: specs, tracking and QA until it ships.
